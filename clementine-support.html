Support - Clementine

Support & Help

Clementine is a GCSE revision planner for iPhone and iPad. It shows you when each exam is, builds a daily and weekly plan for each subject, runs focus sessions with a blooming-flower timer, and lets you design flashcards and share them with friends. This page answers the most common questions and helps you fix problems. If you can't find what you need, get in touch using the details at the bottom.

Getting Started

Initial Setup

  1. Choose a name – Enter a first name or nickname (up to 20 characters). It stays on your devices; friends only see it on decks and progress you choose to send them.
  2. Pick your subjects – Tap the subjects you are taking. You need at least one.
  3. Choose your exam boards – Set the board (and the tier, where there is one) for each subject, then tap Build my plan.
  4. Rate your topics – Open a subject from the Subjects tab and tap one of the five dots next to each topic to say how confident you feel. Pale petals get more revision sessions.
  5. Set your free time – Choose how many minutes you can revise on each day of the week. A day with no time set aside is a rest day.
  6. Start a session – On Today, tap a session to start the flower timer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my name, subjects, boards or tiers?

On Today, tap the gear icon to open Settings. From there you can change your name, your subjects, and the board and tier for each one. If you deselect a subject it is hidden, but its topics and your confidence ratings are kept in case you add it back.

Where do the exam dates come from?

The dates come from the exam boards' published summer 2027 timetables. They are included for each subject's default board (AQA for most subjects, Edexcel for Maths, and OCR for Computer Science). If you take a different board, add its papers yourself with the plus button on the Exams tab. Always check every date against your statement of entry from school, because your board and tier decide which papers you sit.

How do I change or add an exam?

Open the Exams tab. Tap a paper to change its subject, name, date, time (morning or afternoon) and length. Tap the plus button to add a paper of your own. A paper from the timetable can be changed but not removed; a paper you added can be removed.

How does Clementine decide what I should revise?

It looks at how much free time you have each day, when your papers are, how confident you are in each topic, and how long it has been since you last studied it. Topics you feel less sure about, and papers that are coming up sooner, get more sessions. The plan updates by itself when you rate a topic or change your exam dates or free time.

Can I move a session to another day?

Yes. On the Plan tab, drag a session to another day (touch and hold first on iPhone). A session you have moved is pinned, so the planner won't move it again.

What is a rest day?

A rest day is a weekday with no revision time set aside. Nothing is planned on it, and it never breaks your streak.

How is my streak worked out?

Your streak is the number of days in a row on which you did something: finished a focus session or ticked off a session. Rest days, and days that had nothing planned, don't break it, and today doesn't break it until the day is over. Clementine has no leaderboards and never tells you off for missing a day.

How does the focus timer work?

Tap a session on Today (or the start button on a subject) to open the timer. It is a flower with eight petals that open one by one as the session runs, and it blooms when you finish. You can pause, add five minutes, or end early. A session shorter than a minute isn't recorded. When a session finishes it is ticked off on Today. The timer keeps the right time if you switch to another app.

How do I make a flashcard?

Open the Cards tab and tap New card. Write the question and the answer, then choose a colour, a pattern and a writing style, and add stickers from the sticker library. On an iPad with an Apple Pencil you can draw on either side of the card.

How do card reviews work?

The Cards tab shows how many cards are due for review today. During a review you tell Clementine how well you knew each card, and it brings harder cards back sooner and easier cards later. Your results also nudge the shade of the matching topic on its subject flower.

How do I share a deck with a friend?

On the Cards tab, swipe a deck and tap Share. You choose what goes in the file: your drawings, your colours and stickers, and (off unless you switch it on) your confidence ratings for the deck. Then send it with AirDrop, Messages or another way you pick from the share sheet. Your friend gets their own copy and anything they change stays on their device. They need Clementine installed to open it.

Can I print a deck?

Yes. On the share screen, choose Save as a PDF to print. The cards are laid out on A4, a page of fronts followed by a page of backs, with the columns mirrored so they line up when printed double-sided.

What happens when a friend sends an updated version of a deck I already have?

Clementine shows what changed (new, reworded and removed cards) and lets you choose Update my copy or Keep both decks. Updating keeps your own progress and any changes you made to cards your friend didn't touch.

How do study buddies work?

There are no accounts and no chat. On Today, tap the buddies button, then Send my progress to share a picture of your week and a small file. When a friend sends theirs back and you open it in Clementine, you are asked whether to add them as a study buddy. You can send a cheer (YOU GOT THIS, PROUD OF YOU, STUDY TOGETHER? or KEEP GOING) to any buddy. Your grades and confidence ratings are never included.

What are the widgets for?

There are three: a small countdown to your next paper, a medium Today's plan widget with buttons to tick sessions off, and a large Exam season widget listing your next six papers. Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap the plus button and search for Clementine.

How do I turn on the daily reminder?

Open Settings (the gear on Today) and switch on the daily reminder, then choose a time. It is off until you turn it on. Clementine asks for permission to send notifications only at that point, and the reminder only appears on days that have sessions.

Can I use Clementine on both my iPhone and my iPad?

Yes. Sign in to both with the same Apple ID and your plan, subjects, cards and progress stay in step through your own iCloud. Clementine also works fully offline, and when iCloud is off or you're signed out it keeps everything on the device you're using. Settings shows the current iCloud status.

How do I export my data?

Open Settings, go to Your data and tap Export all my data. You get one zip file, shared through the iOS share sheet so it goes nowhere unless you choose where. It contains everything you have made (as a clementine-data.json file) and a PDF of each deck.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Settings, go to Your data and tap Delete all my data. Clementine asks first and explains what will happen: your data is removed from this device and from your iCloud (and so from your other devices). It can't be undone, and it doesn't remove copies friends already have. To clear Clementine's iCloud storage from Apple's side too, go to the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud.

Troubleshooting

My iPad (or iPhone) doesn't show my plan

Issue: You set Clementine up on one device and the other starts from scratch or is missing things.

Solution:

  • Check both devices are signed in with the same Apple ID and are connected to the internet
  • In the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, and check Clementine is switched on
  • Open Clementine and give it a minute; the iCloud status in Settings shows whether it is syncing
  • Make sure iCloud has storage space available

Reminders or notifications don't appear

Issue: The daily reminder never shows up.

Solution:

  • Check the reminder is switched on in Clementine's Settings and set to the time you expect
  • In the iOS Settings app, open Notifications, then Clementine, and allow notifications
  • Remember the reminder only appears on days that have sessions planned
  • Check Do Not Disturb or a Focus mode isn't hiding it

An exam date looks wrong

Issue: A paper is on a different day or time from the one you were told.

Solution:

  • Compare it with your statement of entry from school, which is the one to trust
  • Check the exam board and tier chosen for that subject in Settings
  • Tap the paper on the Exams tab and correct its date, time or length

A friend's deck or progress file won't open

Issue: Tapping a file a friend sent doesn't open it in Clementine.

Solution:

  • Make sure Clementine is installed and up to date on your device
  • Look for a file ending in .clementine (a deck) or .clementine-progress (progress); open it from Messages, AirDrop or Files and choose Clementine
  • Ask your friend to send it again
  • If you can't install Clementine, ask for a PDF of the deck instead

A widget is out of date or blank

Issue: The widget shows old information or no plan.

Solution:

  • Open Clementine once so it can refresh your widgets
  • Check you have finished onboarding and chosen at least one subject
  • Remove the widget and add it again

My Apple Pencil drawing isn't appearing

Issue: Drawing on a card does nothing.

Solution:

  • Drawing is available on iPad only
  • Switch Pencil on in the card maker, and choose an ink colour
  • With Pencil on, only the Pencil draws; your finger still scrolls and selects

App Crashes or Freezes

Issue: Clementine becomes unresponsive or closes unexpectedly.

Solution:

  • Close and relaunch the app
  • Restart your device
  • Update to the latest version of Clementine and of iOS
  • If the problem continues, export your data (Settings, Your data) before contacting support

Features Guide

Today

  • Countdown to your first exam, with a flower that has one petal per subject
  • This week at a glance: done days, today, rest days and what is still to come
  • Today's sessions: tap a row to start the timer, or tap the tick to mark it done
  • Your streak, and cheers from your study buddies

Plan

  • Your whole week, with each session's length shown by its size
  • A summary of planned against done time, and a stacked bar of minutes per subject
  • Drag sessions to other days; moved sessions stay where you put them

Exams

  • A May and June calendar with each exam day filled in the subject's colour
  • A Coming up list with the date, time, length and days to go for each paper
  • Edit any paper or add your own

Subjects

  • A flower per subject, with one petal per topic and its shade showing your confidence
  • Tap the dots to rate each topic from New to Nailed it
  • A Next up topic and a one-tap start button

Focus

  • A flower timer with eight petals that open as the session goes on
  • Pause, add five minutes, or end early
  • Respects Reduce Motion: the flower only grows its petals, with no swaying or glow

Cards and Sharing

  • Design flashcards with colours, patterns, three writing styles, 51 stickers and Apple Pencil drawing on iPad
  • Spaced repetition brings cards back when you need them
  • Share decks as files, or print them as PDFs
  • Send and receive weekly progress with study buddies

Your Data

  • Export everything as a zip, or delete everything, from Settings
  • iCloud status shown in Settings

Tips & Best Practices

Getting the Most from Your Plan

  • Rate your topics honestly. Shaky topics get more sessions, so the plan only works if the ratings are true
  • Update your ratings as you improve and watch the petals deepen
  • Set your real free time for each day, and use rest days so the plan is one you can keep to
  • Short, regular sessions work better than long ones. Pick a session length (15, 20, 25 or 30 minutes) in Settings

Working with Flashcards

  • Keep each card to one question
  • Make cards for the topics you rated lowest first
  • Review the cards that are due each day instead of cramming them all at once

Sharing Safely

  • Only send decks and progress to people you know, using the share sheet
  • Check the switches on the share screen before sending
  • Your confidence ratings are only included in a deck if you switch that on

Backup Recommendations

  • Keep iCloud switched on for Clementine so your data stays in step across your devices
  • Export your data from Settings before you delete the app or switch phones
  • Before using Delete all my data, export first

Good to Know

Clementine is a revision helper, and a few things are worth keeping in mind as you use it.

  • Your plan is a suggestion. Clementine builds it from the time you say you have, your exam dates and how confident you feel, and you can change it whenever you like. It can't promise any particular exam result.
  • Check your exam dates. Dates for each subject's default board come from the exam boards' published timetables, and papers for any other board are ones you enter yourself. Mistakes are possible, so check every date against your statement of entry from school. If anything differs, your statement of entry is the one to trust.
  • Decks from friends are your friends' work. Clementine doesn't check the cards in a deck someone sends you, so check the facts against your notes, your textbook or your teacher before you rely on them.
  • Keep your own copy. Your data lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, and we can't recover it for you because we never have a copy. If iCloud is off and you lose or reset a device, your data goes with it. Use Export all my data in Settings every so often to keep a copy of your own.

Privacy & Data

Clementine asks only for a first name or nickname. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. Your data stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud, which the developer cannot see. The only things that leave your devices are your iCloud sync and the files you choose to share. You have complete control over your data and can export or delete it at any time.
