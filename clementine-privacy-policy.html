Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2 October 2026

In short: Clementine keeps what you put into it on your own iPhone and iPad, and in your own iCloud if you have iCloud switched on. We never receive it, so we can't see it. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only things that leave your devices are your own iCloud sync and the files you choose to send to friends.

Introduction

Clementine ("we," "our," or "the app") is a GCSE revision planner for iPhone and iPad, made by an independent developer, Enrique Cano. It shows your exam dates, builds a daily and weekly study plan, runs focus sessions, and lets you make flashcards and share them with friends. This Privacy Policy explains what the app stores, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Clementine is made for students preparing for their GCSEs, many of whom are under 18. It is designed with UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) and the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) Children's Code in mind: it collects as little as possible, its settings start on the most private option, and nothing in it nudges you to share more.

Information the App Stores

Everything below is something you type, choose or do in the app. It is stored on your device, and in your iCloud (see "iCloud Sync"). We do not receive any of it.

  • Your name: a first name or nickname of up to 20 characters. No surname is asked for.
  • Your subjects: which GCSE subjects you take, and the exam board and tier for each
  • Your confidence ratings: how sure you feel about each topic
  • Your exam dates: the dates of your papers, including any you add or change
  • Your revision plan: your free time for each weekday, your preferred session length, the sessions planned for you, and which ones you have ticked off
  • Your focus sessions: when you studied and for how long
  • Your flashcards: the text, colours, stickers and Apple Pencil drawings on each card, and your card review history
  • Your study buddies: the name, colour and weekly progress that a friend chose to send you, and any cheers you and they have exchanged
  • Your daily reminder time, if you switch the reminder on

Information We Do Not Collect

The app never asks for, and we never receive, your surname, date of birth, email address, school, location, contacts or photos, or any identifier for you or your device. The app does not use the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts or location.

Automatically Collected Information

None. The app has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind.

How the App Uses Your Information

The app uses what it stores only to do what it says:

  1. Planning: working out which topics to revise on which days, from your free time, your exam dates and how confident you feel
  2. Showing your progress: your streak, your week at a glance, the flower for each subject, and the widgets on your Home Screen, which read the same data on your device
  3. Reminders: if you switch on the daily reminder, the app asks iOS to show a notification at the time you choose. It is set up on your device and names that day's subjects. Permission to send notifications is asked for only when you turn the reminder on.
  4. Sharing with friends: only when you choose to, as described below

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Data Storage and Security

On Your Device

Your data is stored on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's SwiftData framework and is protected by iOS's built-in security features. The Clementine widgets read the same data on the same device.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Clementine keeps your iPhone and iPad in step using the private database of your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit). Only you can access it, and we cannot see it. It uses a small part of your iCloud storage and is handled under Apple's privacy policy. iCloud also sends background notifications to your devices to tell them that something has changed. These come from Apple, not from us.

If iCloud is off, or you are signed out, the app works fully and keeps everything on the device you are using. The iCloud status is shown in the app's Settings.

No Servers

We run no servers for the app, and the app does not contact any website. The only network use is Apple's iCloud sync, which you can turn off in the iOS Settings app.

Sharing With Friends

Clementine has no accounts, no public profiles, no search for other people, no chat and no links to web pages. The only way anything reaches a friend is a file or picture that you choose to send, using the iOS share sheet (for example AirDrop or Messages).

Decks (.clementine files)

A deck file contains the deck's title and subject; its cards (the text, plus colours, stickers and drawings if you leave those switches on); your display name as the author and sender; and a random ID that the app made for your installation of Clementine, so that a friend's copy can receive your updates. If you switch on "Include my confidence", it also contains your confidence rating for the deck's topic. That switch is off unless you turn it on.

A deck file never contains your review history, focus sessions, study buddies, plan or exam dates.

Progress (.clementine-progress files and pictures)

When you tap "Send my progress", the app makes a picture of your week (your flower, your name, the time you studied, your streak and petals) and a small file with your display name, your chosen colour, the week it covers, the minutes you studied, your streak, your petals (0 to 8), the time it was sent, and the same kind of random ID. If you send a cheer, it also contains one of four fixed phrases and who it is for.

A progress file never contains your grades, your confidence ratings, which subjects you find hard, or your exam dates.

What Happens Once You Send Something

The person who receives a file has a copy of it, and the service you send it with (such as Messages, Mail or AirDrop) follows its own privacy policy. Deleting your data in Clementine cannot remove copies that friends already have, so please only send to people you know.

What You Receive

When you open a friend's file in Clementine, the app checks it carefully before using it. A new study buddy is added only after you say yes. Cheers are limited to four fixed phrases, so nobody can send you free text. The copy of the file that iOS hands to the app is removed once it has been read.

Your Rights and Choices

Access and Control

  • See your data: everything the app holds is shown in the app
  • Change your data: you can edit or remove your name, subjects, ratings, exam dates, cards and study buddies whenever you like
  • Export your data: in Settings, under Your data, "Export all my data" makes one zip file with everything you have made (a data file and a PDF of each deck). It is shared through the iOS share sheet, so it goes nowhere unless you choose where.
  • Delete your data: in Settings, under Your data, "Delete all my data" asks you first, then removes everything from your device and from your iCloud, so it disappears from your other devices too. It cannot be undone.
  • Remove Clementine's iCloud storage: in the iOS Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud
  • Notifications: you can switch the reminder off in the app, or in the iOS Settings app, at any time

Your Rights Under UK Law

You have rights over your personal information, including the rights to be informed about how it is used, to access it, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its use, and to take a copy with you. Because we hold no copy of your data, the controls above are how you use these rights. If you have a question or a concern, or you would like a parent or guardian to ask on your behalf, please contact us using the details below. You can also complain to the ICO at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

Children's Privacy

Clementine is made for GCSE students, who are usually 14 to 16 and so under 18. For that reason the app asks only for a first name or nickname; it has no advertising, no tracking, no profiling and no public features; and nothing it holds leaves your own devices and iCloud unless you decide to send it.

Parents and guardians can see everything the app holds by looking at it on the student's device, can export or delete it using the buttons in Settings, and can contact us with questions.

Data Retention

The app keeps your data until you delete it. We hold none ourselves. If you use "Delete all my data", it is removed from your device and from your iCloud. Deleting the app from your device does not by itself remove the copy in your iCloud, so use "Delete all my data" first, or remove the storage in the iOS Settings app, under your name, then iCloud.

Third-Party Services

The app uses only Apple's own services and frameworks:

  • iCloud (CloudKit): to keep your iPhone and iPad in step
  • The iOS share sheet, AirDrop, Messages and Mail: when you choose to send a file
  • Local notifications: for the daily reminder, if you switch it on
  • WidgetKit, SwiftData and PencilKit: for the widgets, storage and drawing on cards

These work under Apple's privacy policies and your device settings. No third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or tracking services are used.
